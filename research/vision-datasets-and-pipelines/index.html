Vision datasets and detection pipelines

Large, clean image datasets do not exist for most specialized domains. I build the pipelines that create them, with iterative Grounding DINO detection, SAM and Mask2Former segmentation and VLM completeness checks, and release the resulting datasets with validated measurements.

A continental-scale dataset of ground beetles with high-resolution images and validated morphological trait measurements

S M Rayeed, Mridul Khurana, Alyson East, Isadora E. Fluck, Elizabeth G. Campolongo, Samuel Stevens, Iuliia Zarubiieva, Scott C. Lowe, Michael W. Denslow, Evan D. Donoso, Jiaman Wu, Michelle Ramirez, Benjamin Baiser, Charles V. Stewart, Paula Mabee, Tanya Berger-Wolf, Anuj Karpatne, Hilmar Lapp, Robert P. Guralnick, Graham W. Taylor, Sydne Record
Scientific Data (Nature Portfolio), Data Descriptor

Over 13,200 NEON ground beetles from 30 sites across the continental US and Hawaii, imaged at high resolution with 39,064 elytra measurements. Measurements from images agree closely with calipers on physical specimens.

MEE 2026Under review

From collection trays to AI-ready data: An operational framework for automated entomological specimen processing

S M Rayeed (co-author)
Methods in Ecology and Evolution (under review)

An end-to-end workflow from photographed collection trays to curated, AI-ready specimen data: iterative open-vocabulary detection with VLM completeness checks, scale-bar and color-card localization, automated metadata validation, and curation errors sent back to the biorepository.

Preprint
NeurIPS 2025 Workshop

BeetleFlow: An Integrative Deep Learning Pipeline for Beetle Image Processing

Fangxun Liu, S M Rayeed, Samuel Stevens, Alyson East, + 17 co-authors, Sydne Record, Charles V. Stewart, Wei-Lun Chao
NeurIPS 2025 Workshop on Imageomics

Three stages for tray images of pinned beetles: detect every specimen, crop and sort it with its metadata, and segment it into body parts. Detection matches the exact count on 97.81% of 1,506 NEON trays.

MEE 2025

Optimizing Image Capture for Computer Vision-Powered Taxonomic Identification and Trait Recognition of Biodiversity Specimens

Alyson East, Elizabeth G. Campolongo, Luke Meyers, S M Rayeed, Samuel Stevens, Iuliia Zarubiieva, Isadora E. Fluck, + 21 co-authors, Sydne Record
Methods in Ecology and Evolution, 2025

A practical framework, written by taxonomists, collection managers, ecologists and computer scientists together, for imaging specimens so that computer vision can use them: metadata, positioning, calibration, background, lighting, resolution and file formats.
