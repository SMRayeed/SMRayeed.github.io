Instance-level re-identification

Re-identification asks whether two images show the same individual, not just the same class. I curated the Grevy's Zebra Census Dataset and helped develop multiview spatio-temporal track clustering for re-identification.

CVPR 2025 Workshop

Animal Re-Identification via Multiview Spatio-Temporal Track Clustering

S M Rayeed (co-author)
CVPR 2025 Workshop on CV4Animals

Individual animals are re-identified by clustering tracks across camera views and time instead of matching single frames, using spatio-temporal constraints to keep identities consistent.

Paper
Dataset

GZCD: Grevy's Zebra Census Dataset

Hugging Face, Imageomics, CC BY 4.0

Census imagery of endangered Grevy's zebras for individual re-identification, detection and classification, with initial and final identity metadata. Grevy's zebras are endangered, and stripe-based identification lets a census count individuals without tagging them.
