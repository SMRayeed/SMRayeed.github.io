Fine-grained visual recognition

Fine-grained recognition is where vision models meet classes that differ in sub-millimeter detail, long-tailed label distributions and strong domain shift. I benchmark self-supervised, supervised and vision-language encoders with linear probing, measure sample efficiency, and test transfer from curated images to in-the-wild photographs, using ground beetle species as the test bed.

ICCV 2025 WorkshopFirst author

Fine-Grained Beetle Taxonomy with Vision Models: A Benchmark on Long-Tailed and Domain-Adaptive Classification

S M Rayeed, Alyson East, Samuel Stevens, Sydne Record, Charles V. Stewart
ICCV 2025 Workshop on Computer Vision for Ecology (CV4E)

12 vision and vision-language encoders, linear-probed on about 100K images of 1,769 carabid species from four long-tailed datasets. ViLT reaches 97% genus and 94% species accuracy, half the training data costs little, and lab-to-field transfer drops 37%.
