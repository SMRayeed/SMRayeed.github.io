Datasets

Open datasets I curated or co-curated, with licenses and DOIs. Each links to the research it supports.

Hawaii-beetles

Hugging Face, DOI 10.57967/hf/7272, CC BY 4.0

Pinned carabids from NEON's Hawaii site: 162 group images, 1,614 individual specimen crops and 1,579 trait rows across 14 endemic species, with caliper and image-based elytra measurements.

Open datasetVision datasets and detection pipelines

2018-NEON-beetles

Hugging Face, DOI 10.57967/hf/6890, CC BY-SA 4.0

Ethanol-preserved carabids from 30 NEON sites: 577 group images, 11,654 individual specimen images and 39,064 elytra measurement records, plus segmentation train and test splits.

Open datasetVision datasets and detection pipelines

GZCD: Grevy's Zebra Census Dataset

Hugging Face, CC BY 4.0

Census imagery of endangered Grevy's zebras for individual re-identification, detection and classification, with initial and final identity metadata.

Open datasetInstance-level re-identification

BdSL47

Mendeley Data, DOI 10.17632/pbb3w3f92y.3, CC BY 4.0

47,000 RGB images of 47 Bangla signs from 10 signers, with 470 CSV files of 21 MediaPipe hand keypoints (x, y, depth) per image.

Open datasetSign language recognition

BioVQA benchmark

GitHub

Questions, answer keys and evaluation code for the four BioVQA components: taxonomy, fine-grained attributes, spatial reasoning and re-identification.

Open datasetVisual question answering for biology
