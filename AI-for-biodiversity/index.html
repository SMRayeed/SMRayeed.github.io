Fine-grained AI for biodiversity

Benchmarks, datasets and pipelines for recognizing and measuring organisms from images

Recognition benchmarkImage datasetPipelines

Vision models identify 1,769 ground beetle species at 94% accuracy from curated images, but lose up to 37% in the field. We built the benchmark, the dataset and the pipelines behind that finding.

Biodiversity monitoring depends on identifying and measuring organisms at scale, a task where classes differ in fine detail, labels follow a long tail and field images look nothing like lab images. Ground beetles, sensitive indicators of ecosystem change monitored across the United States by NEON, have all of these properties.

This line of work covers the full path: imaging specimens and validating their measurements, extracting each specimen from tray photographs with open-vocabulary detection and segmentation, and benchmarking which vision encoders can recognize them.

ICCV 2025 WorkshopFirst author

Fine-grained recognition benchmark

12 self-supervised, supervised and vision-language encoders, linear-probed on 100,885 images of 1,769 species from four long-tailed datasets. ViLT reaches 97.15% genus and 93.97% species accuracy; half the training data costs little accuracy; transfer from lab to field images drops by up to 37%.

Scientific Data 2026First author

Continental-scale image dataset with validated traits

13,200+ NEON ground beetles from 30 sites, imaged at high resolution, extracted from group images with Grounding DINO and measured: 39,064 elytra records whose image measurements agree with calipers at R² 0.94 to 0.98.

NeurIPS 2025 Workshop

BeetleFlow: detection, cropping and part segmentation

Three stages for tray images of pinned beetles: detect every specimen, crop and sort it with its metadata, and segment it into body parts. Detection matches the exact count on 97.81% of 1,506 NEON trays.

NeurIPS 2025 Workshop

Mind the (Data) Gap: Evaluating Vision Systems in Small Data Applications

With hundreds to thousands of labels, the regime most science lives in, multimodal LLMs plateau early while vision-only encoders keep improving. On NeWT, 10x more training samples beats 10x more inference compute.
